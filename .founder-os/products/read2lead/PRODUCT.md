# Product — Read2Lead

- Product: Read2Lead
- Slug: read2lead
- Founder: Phuong
- Status: product
- Founder approved: yes
- Created: 2026-07-03

## User

- Primary user: Vietnamese children aged 6–12 learning English
- Payer: Parents, premium families paying for English education

## Problem

- User problem: Children need structured, engaging English reading practice with listening, reading, speaking, and read-aloud activities
- Current workaround: None — Read2Lead is live and serving real users

## Outcome

- Product promise: Interactive 4-phase English reading lessons (listen, read, speak, read aloud) with graded rewards, XP, coins, badges, and rank progression
- Success metric: Active daily usage by enrolled children with measurable reading improvement

## V1 scope

- Included: Story pack generation, guided listening, listening fill-blank, listen-and-order, reading comprehension, listen-and-speak, read-aloud, graded rewards (S/A/B/F), XP/coins/badges, rank tiers, leaderboard, cosmetics shop, student profile
- Not included: Open conversation, AI tutoring, parent dashboard beyond ho-so

## Constraints

- Monthly budget: USD 80 including subscriptions (shared with SpeakUp)
- Production approval: Phuong
- Audience: Real children using the product daily
