## User

- Primary user: Vietnamese children aged 6–12 learning English
- Payer: Parents, premium families paying for English education

## Problem

- User problem: Children need structured, engaging English reading practice with listening, reading, speaking, and read-aloud activities
- Current workaround: None — Read2Lead is live and serving real users

## Outcome

- Product promise: Interactive 4-phase English reading lessons (listen, read, speak, read aloud) with graded rewards, XP, coins, badges, and rank progression

- Not included: Open conversation, AI tutoring, parent dashboard beyond ho-so
