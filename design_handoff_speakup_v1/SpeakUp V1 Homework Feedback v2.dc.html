Encourage mood — lower match %, same warm structure

The sandwich keeps the exact same 4-part structure — never softer copy that reads as pity, never omitted just because the score is low (per V1-D1: the sandwich renders after every scored attempt, subject to grounding validation).

03 · Fix-it round — start

Invitation — skippable, never forced

After the feedback sandwich (or after end-of-set), an invitation card offers ≤3 reps of missed/focus words only. A kid can skip straight to "Bước tiếp theo" — the fix-it round never blocks progress.

04 · Fix-it round — rep screen

Word large · compare · re-record

One word per rep, shown large. Two compare buttons let the kid hear Minny's model and their own last recording side-by-side before re-recording. Re-record reuses the standard spk-rec-btn at a smaller size appropriate to this focused context.
